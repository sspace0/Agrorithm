## Scope and attribution notes

- POWER supplies genuine historical 2018–2024 weather for one regional point; HLS L30 v2.0 supplies a separately dated 2023 vegetation-context series. The listed sources do not establish a forecast or a field-scale agronomic recommendation.
- The Köppen-Geiger input is only the historical 1991–2020 provider 0.5° layer. The future projections named in the paper title are not used.
- Crop-reference links are the actual sources embedded in the current crop profiles and atlas. Regional transfer and thresholds retain the limitations shown in the application.
- Blue Marble and Earth with Clouds are historical presentation assets. The separate crop, root-zone and groundwater models are original procedural Blender work; external PBR textures are credited above. Exact texture-map download URLs, creators, hashes and modifications remain in assets/textures/manifest.json.
- The project-supplied Agrorithm wordmarks, Paint.NET source, report cover and background have no external source URL supplied. They are preserved in assets/branding and assets/textures, with provenance in their manifests. No external author or open license is invented. Century Gothic is an installed-system font preference; its files are not redistributed. DM Sans and Instrument Serif are the bundled fonts.
- Direct runtime and development packages are listed above. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt and pnpm-lock.yaml preserve the complete installed transitive dependency and license inventory; not every transitive package is a separate form row.
- Live HTTP checks on 15 November 2026 succeeded for all 18 NASA rows and 63 of the 78 other rows. The remaining 15 URLs returned access blocks, connection/time limits or a server error; none returned 404. Their exact recorded source URLs are retained rather than replaced with an unrelated source. The local check record is output/judging-audit/submission-url-checks.json; successful reachability does not independently validate a scientific claim.
- The inspected evidence includes docs/DATA_REGISTRY.md, docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md, docs/ATLAS.md, src/data/crops.ts, src/data/cropProfiles.ts, src/data/atlas.ts, public/data/provenance.json, public/data/vegetation.json, public/data/basemap-provenance.json, public/data/climate-zones.json, asset/font manifests, package.json, and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt. Source URLs are copied from these records or the corresponding official project metadata.
