# Five-minute walkthrough

1. Open `/FieldShift/`. The poster appears immediately and the GLB replaces it when ready. Orbit the illustrative cutaway or select Static to use the whole planner without 3D.
2. Try a sample farm. Explain that observations are genuine and regional, while baseline, soil and management are demonstration inputs. No real farmer interview is claimed.
3. Evidence: inspect seven POWER years and 43 HLS scenes, gaps, valid counts and source granules. Open the accessible tables. Low NDVI does not establish bare soil or absent residue.
4. Rotation lab: compare aligned 36-month calendars. Inspect provisional crop parameters, exact dates, residue assumptions and crop-stage exposure.
5. What if: lower rainfall and increase temperature, then reduce available water. Water demand and feasibility recompute. These are hypothetical stress tests, not forecasts.
6. Trade-offs: inspect metrics, Pareto eligibility, four historical replays, optional priorities and the six sensitivity cases. The cover can use more water than baseline; it is not forced to win.
7. Your plan: print or export JSON/CSV, save locally, resume and delete. No private field data is published to GitHub.
8. Soil explorer: switch root/groundwater, inspect layers and processes. The depth slider is illustrative, with a scale break; it is not a measured aquifer forecast.

Concept routes: `#/studio/lab` (default), `#/console/lab`, `#/companion/lab`. Complete workflows use the same six route suffixes: setup, evidence, lab, scenarios, tradeoffs, export. Separate review route: `#/design`.
